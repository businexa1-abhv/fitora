Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the verification process take? expand_more
Most venues are verified and ready to host within 48-72 business hours. Our team reviews your facility details and safety standards to ensure the best experience for our athletes.
Do I need special hardware to manage bookings? expand_more
No special hardware is required. You can manage everything through our web portal or mobile app on any smartphone, tablet, or computer with an internet connection.
When and how do I receive my payments? expand_more
Payments are processed instantly upon booking. Funds are typically disbursed to your linked business bank account every Friday for the preceding week's earnings.
